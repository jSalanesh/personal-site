About Me

Hi, I'm Joseph - a passionate software developer with a love for Python. I enjoy creating practical tools that solve real-world problems, like automating tasks for cognitive behavioral therapy.

Before diving into software development, I spent two years as a Virtual Assistant for an AAMCO Transmission repair shop. My responsibilities included ordering parts, scheduling appointments for customers, calling warranty companies to file claims on behalf of customers, and using Google Sheets to track inventory. This experience sharpened my organizational skills and taught me how technology can streamline business operations — which ultimately led me to pursue a career in software development.

My skills include building GUI applications, web scraping, and automation. While I'm still growing my experience, I'm an eager learner who thrives on tackling new challenges and expanding my knowledge in software development. Let's connect and build something great together!
